# Crash Rebound — Frozen Technical Trigger for True OOS Validation

Frozen before viewing 2025 validation outcomes.

## Mechanical event screen
1. Crash magnitude >= 30% down.
2. Crash-day relative volume >= 5x the prior-20-session median.
3. RSP prior-5-session return > -1%.

## Day-1 technical confirmation
4. Day-1 low holds the crash-day low.
5. Day-1 close is above the crash-day midpoint.
6. Day-1 opening gap versus crash-day close is greater than -5%.

## Execution
- Entry: Day-1 close.
- Primary evaluation exit: Day-10 close.
- Secondary reporting horizons: Day-3 and Day-5 closes.
- No headline/news classification is used in the trigger.
- No threshold or rule changes are permitted after 2025 outcomes are viewed.

## Validation period
Calendar year 2025 only. No 2025 data was used in development or targeted expansion.

## Pre-specified interpretation
The rule is considered supported only if the untouched sample shows:
- positive median post-entry return by Day 10,
- more than 50% positive trades by Day 10,
- no single month is solely responsible for the positive aggregate,
- and downside is not obviously worse than the development sample.

Failure on these conditions means the frozen rule is not validated and must not be promoted as a usable trigger without a new, separately documented hypothesis.
