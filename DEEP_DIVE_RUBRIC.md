# 30-Day Rocket Screen — Deep-Dive Rubric

Locked before the Aug. 30, 2026 candidate deep dive. Apply identically to every quantitative candidate before any FINAL #1/#2/#3 ranking.

## 100-point evidence score
- Growth / operating acceleration: 15
- Guidance + beat/raise execution: 15
- Estimate-revision direction: 10
- Credible next-30-day catalyst: 12
- Current technical condition + SPY/peer relative strength: 15
- Valuation / upside runway: 10
- Balance sheet + financing/dilution quality: 8
- Sector / industry support: 5
- Downside/risk quality: 10

## Evidence rules
- Use fresh/current evidence; primary company filings/IR are preferred for company facts.
- Unavailable evidence receives no invented favorable score and reduces evidence confidence.
- No identified catalyst is not an automatic exclusion; it simply receives no catalyst bonus.
- Recent negative price action must be explicitly tested for fundamental justification.
- Every candidate gets the same checklist and scoring categories.

## Hard-risk treatment
Normally exclude from FINAL ranking when the 30-day thesis is predominantly dependent on:
- a binary clinical/regulatory outcome;
- a crypto/digital-asset treasury proxy rather than operating-company execution;
- shell/SPAC-like mechanics;
- imminent financing/dilution that materially overwhelms the operating thesis.

Commercial healthcare/biotech companies are not automatically excluded; the test is whether the 30-day thesis is predominantly binary.

## Finalization gate
No FINAL #1/#2/#3 until all 25 candidates have current verification status, evidence confidence, risk classification, and advance/exclude/watch rationale recorded, followed by a direct comparison of the surviving finalists.
