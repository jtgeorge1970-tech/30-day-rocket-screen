# ENGINE 4 SSOT v5.7 — Top-25 Multi-Day Bench Addendum

Effective: 2026-09-18
Repository: `jtgeorge1970-tech/30-day-rocket-screen`

This addendum preserves strong B-or-better candidates that are not yet ripe
without allowing an unbounded watch list or weakening any entry gate.

## Capacity and tiers

- The Bench is capped at 25. This is a configured cap, never a survivor count.
- Rank 1–5: **Hot**. Only these five Bench names reach the 09:45 live setup check.
- Rank 6–15: **Developing**.
- Rank 16–25: **Reserve**.
- Fewer than 25 may be retained when fewer names qualify naturally.

## Daily admission, competition, and removal

Every market day, yesterday's Bench and today's frozen launchpad compete again
using fresh price, activity, catalyst, spread, volatility, room, and eligibility
evidence. A candidate is admitted when it passes all current mandatory gates and:

1. has a current score of at least 85; or
2. is in today's launchpad Top 3 with a current score of at least 80; or
3. was already benched and remains current B grade or better (score at least 80).

A name is removed immediately when it falls below B, fails a mandatory gate,
lacks trustworthy current evidence, reaches more than five completed watch
sessions, or is displaced by a stronger name at the Top-25 cap.

## Trade safety

The Bench is observation, not trade approval. Every Hot candidate must pass the
same fresh 09:45 live-entry checks as today's launchpad. A prior BUY or ARM signal
is never reused. If price falls or a setup fails, the candidate must form and pass
a new setup; the engine must not chase or submit a stale order. The official
trade-price cap remains $100. Higher-priced names may appear only as explicitly
labeled research/shadow observations and can never produce an official order.

## Required reporting

Each automatic and manual live run reports, separately:

- Bench competition universe;
- naturally watch-qualified;
- retained by the Top-25 Bench cap;
- Hot, Developing, and Reserve counts/tickers;
- additions, promotions, demotions, removals, and exact removal reasons;
- Bench live-check result separately from selection, primary entry, recovery,
  and execution results.

All counts are date-locked to the active Eastern market date. Missing values are
reported as unavailable and are never inferred.
