# 30-Day Rocket Screen — Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

## Absolute governing rule — no shortcuts
This rule overrides speed, convenience, partial results, provisional rankings, and any temptation to infer completion from incomplete work.

Every official Rocket Screen run must execute the entire locked process from the beginning through the final verified ranking. No stage may be skipped, abbreviated, sampled, substituted, assumed, or declared complete without evidence that it was actually completed.

The assistant has no authority to shorten or bypass this process unless the user explicitly changes the SSOT. A quantitative shortlist is never permission to jump directly to a FINAL ranking. If any required stage is incomplete, the status must be stated as incomplete and no official #1/#2/#3 may be declared.

## Objective
Identify the U.S.-listed stock(s) with the strongest probability-weighted setup for a gain of roughly +10% over the next ~30 days, without pretending that unavailable or unverified data has been checked.

The long-run objective is positive expectancy, not winning every trade: control losses, rotate dead money, protect profitable positions, and allow exceptional winners to continue running so that average gains and win rate can exceed average losses over a sufficiently large sample.

## Non-negotiable quality rule
No stock may be labeled the official FINAL #1/#2/#3 unless the broad eligible universe has been systematically processed through the quantitative funnel and the surviving candidates have then been verified with fresh/current company-specific information.

A market-wide quantitative score is not a FINAL recommendation.

## Funnel
1. Build the broad U.S.-listed common-stock/ADR universe.
2. Remove ETFs, test issues, warrants, rights, units, preferreds, obvious SPAC/acquisition shells and other non-common-stock structures.
3. Obtain market-wide price/volume history and calculate investability metrics.
4. Require basic investability: price >= $3, average dollar volume >= $2M/day, market cap >= $200M.
5. Score every sufficiently supported eligible stock under three independent quantitative lenses.
6. Apply a data-quality/confidence gate. Missing secondary information reduces confidence; it must not be silently converted into favorable evidence.
7. Narrow to approximately 100 semifinalists and then approximately 20-30 quantitative candidates.
8. Perform deeper current research only on the narrowed candidate set: latest earnings, guidance, estimate revisions, beat/raise execution, identified catalysts, financing/dilution, binary/regulatory risk, downside, valuation/runway, and current technical condition.
9. Narrow to a small finalist group and compare directly.
10. Apply the Crown Jewel finalist analysis defined below.
11. Only after current verification and Crown Jewel analysis may #1/#2/#3 be labeled FINAL.
12. Before deploying new capital, apply the Market Regime Gate and the pre-purchase execution check.

## Three quantitative lenses
### 1. Leadership / Momentum
Looks for sustained relative leadership, healthy intermediate momentum, sector/industry strength, growth quality, liquidity and reasonable valuation/runway.

### 2. Beaten-Down / Reversal
Looks for meaningful drawdown combined with improving short-term trend, volume confirmation, fundamental support and adequate liquidity. A falling stock is not rewarded merely for being down.

### 3. Acceleration / Emerging Momentum
At scale the engine detects fingerprints of an emerging move: acceleration in returns, abnormal volume, improving growth/earnings characteristics, sector/industry confirmation and relative strength. It does NOT claim a catalyst has been found. A real catalyst is researched only after the universe has been narrowed.

## Market-wide data that may be used
Use only information that can be collected consistently enough across thousands of securities: price/volume history, dollar liquidity, market cap, revenue and earnings growth, valuation where available, analyst/target information where available, sector/industry classification and relative performance, SPY-relative performance, drawdown/reversal, moving-average position and volatility. Earnings dates may be used when reliably obtainable, but absence must not eliminate an otherwise well-supported stock.

## Missing-data rule
Core investability data is mandatory. Secondary fields are optional. Do not fill missing secondary factors with arbitrary low/medium scores and then treat them as actual evidence. Scores must use available factors with weight re-normalization, and each stock must receive explicit `data_confidence`. A stock with insufficient factor coverage cannot advance.

## Catalyst rule
No catalyst found does NOT automatically eliminate a stock. Confirmed positive catalyst is positive evidence; exceptional quant/fundamental evidence may remain eligible without catalyst bonus; ambiguous catalyst gets uncertainty penalty; negative catalyst may eliminate; predominantly binary clinical/regulatory dependence normally eliminates under risk rules.

## Risk rules
Finalist verification must explicitly test binary clinical/regulatory dependence, financing/dilution, shell/SPAC characteristics, extreme crypto-proxy dependence, deteriorating guidance/fundamentals, whether negative price action is justified, excessive extension/volatility, and downside relative to the expected ~30-day opportunity.

## Freshness and relative-strength rules
A new full run must not silently reuse stale fundamentals. Cached/checkpoint fundamentals require timestamps and expiration; stale rows refresh. Use both SPY-relative strength and peer context; industry strength should be used when enough peers exist.

## Final research requirements
For EACH quantitative candidate considered for FINAL ranking, complete the same fresh/current checklist: latest earnings/operating trends, guidance/change, revisions, beat/raise execution, credible next-30-day catalyst if any, current technical condition/extension, sector/industry strength, valuation/runway, downside/risk, financing/dilution/special situation, and whether recent negative price action is fundamentally justified. Unfinished candidates cannot justify FINAL ranking.

## Crown Jewel finalist analysis — mandatory final layer
After the full universe screen, full candidate deep dive and Top 3 selection, perform one equal Crown Jewel analysis. This never replaces an earlier stage.

Crown Jewel weighting:
- **Catalyst / Story — 50%**
- **Full Screen Technical + Fundamental Evidence — 30%**
- **Chart / Price Setup — 20%**

Catalyst/Story judges strength, freshness, credibility, timing and potential next-30-day magnitude. Full Screen carries forward verified growth, execution, revisions, RS, sector support, valuation/runway, balance sheet, dilution, liquidity, downside and confidence. Chart/Price Setup inspects multi-timeframe support/resistance, ranges, breakout/reversal, recovery, volume, extension, consolidation/pullback and realistic room to +10%.

Calculate all Top 3 under the same evidence standard. Highest Crown Jewel score becomes the preferred single-stock candidate for the ~$300 30-day experiment, subject to the Market Regime Gate and final execution check. Larger diversified allocations may separately use Top 3/broader finalists but do not change the measured single-stock experiment unless explicitly changed.

## Market Regime Gate — mandatory before new capital
Stock selection and market exposure are separate decisions. A strong individual Rocket is not sufficient reason to deploy new long capital when the broad market is entering a materially hostile regime.

Before every new Rocket purchase, classify the broad U.S. equity environment using current evidence including SPY, QQQ and Russell/small-cap trend; market breadth and participation; major moving-average/trend structure; volatility; credit/risk appetite where useful; sector leadership; and evidence of broad deterioration or recovery. Do not call a bear market from one down day or one indicator.

### GREEN — normal/bull/risk-on
Broad trend and participation are sufficiently healthy. Normal Rocket deployment is permitted, subject to individual risk and execution checks.

### YELLOW — deteriorating/uncertain
Evidence is mixed or worsening. New exposure should be reduced or require materially stronger setups; cash allocation may rise; existing positions continue to follow their individual stops and thesis rules. Document why the regime is Yellow.

### RED — confirmed bear/severe risk-off
Broad evidence shows a sustained hostile regime. Stop initiating ordinary long Rocket positions. Existing long positions continue under their individual stops/thesis-break rules; do not automatically liquidate a protected runner solely because the label changed unless its risk/reward no longer justifies holding. Undeployed capital may remain in cash.

### Bearish/inverse strategy separation
Cash is a valid position. An inverse-index or short strategy may be researched and activated later, but it is a SEPARATE strategy and must not be automatically substituted for long Rockets. Before using inverse exposure, define and validate its own instrument selection, entry, exit, holding-period, sizing and risk rules. Leveraged inverse ETFs require special caution because daily reset/compounding can materially affect multi-day returns. Until a bearish strategy is separately validated and explicitly activated, RED means primarily capital preservation/cash rather than automatic inverse buying.

Record the market-regime classification and evidence at each new entry and at each 30-day review so later performance can be analyzed by regime.

## Pre-purchase execution check
Immediately before purchase, refresh current/premarket price and gap, material overnight/company news, market conditions/regime and thesis-changing developments. Do not automatically rerun the full universe merely because a new trading day began. If nothing material changed, preserve the Crown Jewel decision; if material change occurred, document and reassess the Top 3 under the same framework.

## Mandatory downside protection — disaster stop
Every Rocket position must have downside protection. Standard initial hard disaster stop is approximately **10% below actual purchase price**, intended to prevent an ordinary ~$300 position from becoming a 30%, 40%, 50%+ loss. It is maximum intended price-based loss under normal continuous trading, not a prediction and not a requirement to wait for -10% before selling.

Before purchase calculate recent realized volatility/typical daily and intraday range and inspect technical support. Confirm the stop is not inside ordinary noise; if 10% is structurally inappropriate, document and reassess rather than silently removing protection.

A position may be sold BEFORE the disaster stop if the thesis materially breaks (guidance cut, financing/dilution, accounting, regulatory/company event, catalyst failure, etc.). Never move the disaster stop downward merely to avoid a loss. Stop orders do not guarantee price; gaps, halts, volatility and liquidity can cause execution below the trigger.

## Multi-position lifecycle and independent 30-day clocks
The strategy may have several Rocket positions operating simultaneously. Each position is managed independently from its own actual entry date and price. Portfolio positions must never be forced onto one common 30-day clock.

At any moment the portfolio may legitimately contain:
- positions stopped out before 30 days;
- positions still between approximately -10% and +10% and awaiting their individual 30-day review;
- positions that achieved +10% and are continuing as runners under profit protection/trailing stops;
- newly opened positions with a fresh 30-day clock;
- cash created by exits or by a Yellow/Red market regime.

### Stop-out
If the active stop triggers, sell and record actual exit date, price and realized return. The slot/capital may later be redeployed through the current Rocket process and Market Regime Gate.

### Sideways / unresolved at ~30 days
At each position's individual ~30-day checkpoint, first record its exact 30-day return. If it remains below +10% and above its disaster stop, do not simply leave dead money indefinitely. Rerun the full Rocket Screen fresh from zero; the incumbent must compete with all new candidates under the same locked methodology. Keep it only if it again earns the position as the strongest probability-weighted next opportunity; otherwise sell/rotate.

### Successful runner / trailing protection
Once a position reaches the +10% experiment objective, record that achievement. Do not automatically cap a strong winner at +10%. Protect the gain and allow continued upside using an appropriate trailing stop, normally approximately 7-10% below the subsequent peak unless current volatility/structure supports a documented adjustment. Continue updating peak and active stop. A +20%, +30%, +40%+ runner is desirable when earned by the market. Never lower a trailing stop merely to avoid an exit.

### Portfolio principle
The system is designed for positive expectancy across many trades, not perfection on every stock. Controlled losers, rotated sideways positions and uncapped-but-protected runners are all expected parts of the strategy. Performance must be evaluated across a sufficiently large sample using win rate, average winner, average loser, expectancy, drawdown and regime context.

Maintain a spreadsheet/ledger as the operational source for overlapping Rocket positions. Every position must track at minimum:

- shares and cost basis
- current price/return
- individual 30-day review date and days held
- +10% target/achievement date
- initial ~-10% disaster stop
- peak price
- trailing-stop percentage and active stop
- next required action/instruction
- exit date/price
- realized return
- market regime at exit/review
- notes including thesis-break events or exceptions

The ledger should make upcoming sales/reviews and active stop instructions visible so multiple overlapping positions cannot be confused.
